中文

本扩展是一款在你自己的浏览器里运行的商品数据处理工具。它的核心设计是没有服务器：不注册、不登录、不需要你提供店铺 API 密钥，没有统计或广告脚本，也不主动连接除商品页之外的任何地址。

一、本扩展不收集、不上传的任何东西

  • 不收集身份信息：不需要邮箱、手机号、账号，扩展内没有任何会让你提交这些的表单。
  • 不上传商品数据：你在 1688 / 速卖通商品页上看到的标题、价格、变体、图片地址，全部在你自己的浏览器内存里解析。
  • 不上传店铺数据：导出的 CSV 与图片 zip 是你主动下载到本机的文件，不经过任何第三方。
  • 不读取你的登录态、Cookie、站内消息或支付信息；不修改你在源站发出的任何请求。
  • 没有埋点、没有分析、没有广告、没有跨站追踪。

二、这个扩展会发出哪些请求

它不发起任何"把数据发给某个服务"的请求，也不查询汇率、天气、地址库之类的第三方接口。全部网络行为只有三类，前两类其实不是扩展的行为：

English

This extension is a product-data tool that runs entirely inside your browser. It has no server: no sign-up, no login, no store API keys, no analytics or advertising code, and no contact with any address other than the product page you are already viewing.

1. What is never collected or uploaded

  • No identity data — it never asks for or stores your email, phone number or credentials.
  • No product data is uploaded: titles, prices, variants and image addresses on a 1688 / AliExpress page are parsed in your own browser's memory.
  • No store data is uploaded: the exported CSV and image zip are files you explicitly choose to download to your computer.
  • Your login state, cookies, messages and payment information are not read, and no request you send to the marketplace is modified.
  • No telemetry, no analytics, no ads, no cross-site tracking.

2. Which requests this extension makes

It never sends your data to a service, and it does not call any third-party API — not even for exchange rates. Every network action falls into one of three rows, and the first two are not the extension's doing:

RequestWho starts itDestinationPayload
Loading a product pageYou (typing a URL or clicking a link)1688 / AliExpressExactly the requests your browser would send anyway
Reading the product data on that pageThe extension, in memory, creating no request—Nothing leaves the tab
Saving a local image backupOnly when you click "pack images"That page's own public image URLsGET, sent with credentials: 'omit' — no cookies, no session, no API key

About the third row: the image rows in the CSV point at the marketplace's public image URLs, so Shopify fetches them from its own servers during import. You only press that button when you want a copy on your own disk in case Shopify cannot pull them; the extension then simply uses your browser to retrieve the images already shown on your screen, three at a time, skipping any it cannot fetch rather than hammering the source.

Nothing else is ever contacted. You can verify this in your browser's network panel while browsing several product pages: no unfamiliar third-party domain appears.

3. Data that stays on your device

chrome.storage.sync holds your pricing parameters (freight rate, expected profit, margin, settlement currency, the exchange rate you type in yourself and the day you typed it, image-filter preferences, interface language). chrome.storage.local holds your "bestsellers" notebook — up to 200 records of products you exported (title, source URL, price, cost, margin, image count), deletable one by one or in full from the popup. Chrome clears both areas on uninstall.

The exchange rate is the one number in this tool that is neither yours nor something visible on the page, so it is only ever what you type: the panel shows the date you entered it and turns red once it is more than 7 days old. The extension does not look rates up and does not receive them from anywhere. None of the above is transmitted to us or to any third party; storage.sync is Google's Chrome Sync mechanism, governed by Chrome's own privacy notice.

4. Images in the exported file

The image rows in the CSV reference the marketplace's own public image URLs so that Shopify can fetch them during import; when you import, Shopify's servers request those URLs. You may optionally save local copies as a zip — that action stays on your computer.

5. Children

Intended for e-commerce professionals; not directed at children, and no personal information is knowingly collected.
